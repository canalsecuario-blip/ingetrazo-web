It's open-source software. Help us keep it going.

The complete app, open source: no locked features, no expiry, and the code published. If it helps you in your work, your contribution keeps it going.

I'm Marco Sumari, a civil engineer and GNU/Linux user. I develop IngeTrazo in my evenings and weekends, and I share it freely so anyone can model and draw without paying for licenses.

IngeTrazo isn't alone: in the same family we're developing IngeCAD, 2D drafting with add-ons for civil engineering (surveying and terrain today; later roads, drainage, canals…), and IngePresupuestos, construction cost estimating. The whole ecosystem was born as a free alternative so that GNU/Linux users like us have tools to work with, and today it also runs on Windows. A contribution here supports all three.

If it saved you hours of work, help us keep maintaining and improving them.
