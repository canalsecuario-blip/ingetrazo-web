Everything you need to model, tag and draw

Draw as if by hand

Draw lines, rectangles, circles and arcs; push a face and you have a solid. No layers of CAD complexity between your idea and the model.

  • Push/Pull, Follow Me, Offset — the SketchUp workflow you already know
  • Exact measurements from the keyboard — type 3.50 and the line is 3.50 m long
  • Smart inferences — axes, extensions, parallels and perpendiculars
  • Materials, textures and dimensions on any face

BIM when you need it, freedom always

BIM in IngeTrazo isn't a cage of rigid families: you model freely and tag whatever you want as a wall, slab, column or door.

  • Live quantity takeoff — m² of wall, m³ of column, door counts
  • Tag as you draw — turn on a class and everything you draw takes it
  • Validated IFC 4 export — opens in any BIM viewer or software
  • Quantities as CSV — your takeoff table in one click

Solids guaranteed watertight: if an operation would break the volume, the engine rejects it instead of corrupting your takeoff.

The terrain is where it starts

Place your project in the real world: satellite imagery, 3D relief and field data, right inside the modeler.

  • 3D terrain with satellite imagery — real elevations of your site
  • Longitudinal profile — draw a route and see its profile instantly
  • Drone survey (WebODM) — your textured photogrammetry mesh as a reference, with real elevations
  • GPS / total station points — import your CSV in UTM and draw on exact points
  • UTM WGS84 throughout the interface — type the Zone/E/N just as your instrument reports it
  • Project north — draw square to the axes; the map, the terrain and the shadows rotate underneath, as in SketchUp
  • Georeferenced routes and boundaries, with chainages and areas

The whole modeler works offline; only the map uses the internet.

From the model to the printed drawing

Compose sheets as in QGIS or LayOut: views of the model at exact scale on the sheet, dimensions that re-measure themselves, and vector PDF ready for the project file.

  • True scale, guaranteed — 1:100 in a 200 mm frame is 20 m of model, always
  • Dimensions anchored to the model — edit the wall and the dimension moves and re-measures itself; chained dimensions with their total, and level marks that read the height of the point
  • Exact hidden-line vector style — three pens (cut, profiles, edges) and poché in sections: the drawing a draftsperson would ink
  • Numbered view titles, section marks and detail callouts — the plan shows where section A-A cuts and which sheet enlarges each detail
  • Editable title block, graphic scale and north arrow — and several sheets per document, to PDF in one click
  • DXF to IngeCAD — the vector view, with one layer per line class, ready to finish the 2D details in the ecosystem's free CAD

And there’s more

Model with AI, inside the app or from Claude

Describe what you want —or attach a photo— and the AI writes the recipe: revolves, extrusions, Python over IngeTrazo's action API. The deterministic engine runs it: every step is one undo, every recipe goes through the watertightness guard, and the model is still yours, editable by hand.

  • Built-in AI Assistant (Extensions ▸ AI Assistant, Ctrl+Shift+A) — a chat with your own key: Groq (with a free tier), Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, DeepSeek or a local model with Ollama. No accounts, no middlemen
  • It sees what it does — it gets viewport screenshots and corrects based on what it sees; with Photo… it rebuilds what an image shows, using the measurements you give it
  • MCP bridge for Claude Desktop and Claude Code — the agent draws, queries and sees the model live, in the open app; one undo step per action and, if its code fails, the document comes back intact

Populating a scene shouldn't cost you the afternoon

A model makes sense once it has people next to it, a tree and a car. IngeTrazo brings 1,510 components one click away and 427 textures — and each one arrives at its real size, not whatever size it happens to get.

  • 1,510 models online — furniture, doors, windows, trees, vehicles and people. Browsing costs kilobytes: only the one you click is downloaded, and it's kept
  • Offline too — eight models and six scale figures come inside the program
  • 427 textures at their real size — a brick looks like brick because the catalog says how big it is, not because you eyeballed it

All the content comes from the free Sweet Home 3D library (CC0, CC BY and Free Art License); IngeTrazo always shows the license and author of each piece.

Open and save your SketchUp files

Your years-long library doesn't get left behind — and neither do your colleagues: IngeTrazo opens .skp directly and saves to .skp whatever you model, thanks to OpenSKP, the free reader/writer for the SketchUp format — no converters, no extra installs.

  • Native SketchUp (.skp) import — every version, from the old ones (2013) to the current ones
  • With everything inside — groups, components, materials, textures, layers, scenes, dimensions and leader texts
  • Export to .skp — your model opens in SketchUp with its groups, named materials, dimensions and texts

How to install ▾

Install it from our repository and flatpak update will bring you every new version. It lands in your applications menu, with .igz and .skp files associated.

flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists ingetrazo https://ingetrazo.com/ingetrazo.flatpakrepo flatpak install --user ingetrazo com.ingetrazo.IngeTrazo flatpak run com.ingetrazo.IngeTrazo

Prefer the standalone file? The card above downloads it and it installs with a double-click, but that way it doesn't update itself.

Installs nothing: make it executable and open it (or double-click it in your file manager).

chmod +x IngeTrazo-0.5.4-x86_64.AppImage ./IngeTrazo-0.5.4-x86_64.AppImage

It needs FUSE, which almost every distro ships. If yours doesn't, use the tar.gz.

For distros without FUSE, or to install it under /opt: extract and run.

tar -xzf IngeTrazo-0.5.4-linux-x86_64.tar.gz IngeTrazo-0.5.4/ingetrazo

It carries the same as the AppImage: Python, Qt and the .skp reader travel inside.

All three run on Ubuntu 22.04 or later. If something fails, --check tells you what's missing.

You can also run it from source (3 commands, see the README). All files are in the GitHub releases.

macOS · Apple Silicon
IngeTrazo for Mac (.dmg) Apple Silicon · macOS

Open the .dmg and drag IngeTrazo to Applications. It isn't signed with an Apple account yet, so the first time macOS won't open it with a double-click: right-click ▸ Open and confirm. From then on it opens normally, and .igz and .skp files open with a double-click. Packaged by Félix Riestra.

For Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 and later). What doesn't work on Mac yet: DWG import and the skp2dae helper converter; opening and saving .skp does. If something fails, tell us in a GitHub issue.

Frequently asked questions

What everyone asks before drawing their first line.

Is it really free?

Yes. IngeTrazo is free software under the GPL-3.0: free today and forever, with its source code published on GitHub. No accounts, no subscriptions, no locked features.

How is it different from SketchUp?

Drawing feels just as direct (push/pull, inferences, exact measurements typed on the keyboard), but IngeTrazo is free, runs natively on Linux, guarantees watertight solids fit for quantity takeoff, and adds the BIM layer: tag the geometry and get quantities and IFC.

Can I open my SketchUp models? And save them?

Yes to both, .skp directly with no converters: File ▸ Import ▸ SketchUp to open, and File ▸ Export ▸ SketchUp to save. Built on OpenSKP, the free reader/writer for the format that IngeTrazo contributes to — it reads files from every version (geometry, groups, components, materials, textures, layers, scenes, dimensions and leader texts), and what you export opens in SketchUp with its annotations included. It also imports COLLADA (.dae) and OBJ.

What's all this about BIM and IFC?

BIM means giving the model meaning: this solid IS a wall, this face IS a door. With that, IngeTrazo calculates construction quantities (m², m³, units) and exports them to IFC, the standard format that cost-estimating software understands — including IngePresupuestos.

Can I produce the project drawings?

Yes. The sheet composer places views of the model at exact scale (1:50, 1:100…) on A4–A0 sheets, with dimensions anchored to the model that update when the model changes, an editable title block, graphic scale, numbered view titles, section marks, level and chained dimensions, detail callouts, and a vector hidden-line style with pens and poché. Export each sheet — or the whole set — to vector PDF, and the view to DXF.

Can I add features with plugins?

Yes. Since version 0.3.2 IngeTrazo has an Extensions menu: copy a plugin (a Python file) into your personal folder and its tools show up in the menu — much like SketchUp extensions. Two come built in: Model Info (statistics, materials with painted m², and BIM objects) and a Python Console to inspect and automate the model with code, all tied into undo. The guide is in the manual (Spanish).

Does it have artificial intelligence?

Yes, in two ways. The built-in AI Assistant is a chat: paste the key of whichever provider you prefer (Groq has a free tier; also Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, or Ollama on your own machine) and describe the model in words or with a photo. And the MCP bridge lets Claude Desktop or Claude Code draw directly in the open app. In both cases the AI generates recipes that the engine executes —never blind meshes—, every step can be undone and the model stays editable by hand. Without AI, IngeTrazo works just the same, and offline.

Does it work offline?

Yes, the whole modeler works offline and your files live on your computer. Only the satellite map and the 3D terrain download imagery from the internet while you use them.

How do I install it?

On Windows: download the installer from the Download section, double-click and you're done (it associates .igz files). On Linux: download the Flatpak (double-click and GNOME Software / KDE Discover installs it, with .igz and .skp files associated), the AppImage (make it executable and open it) or, if your distro lacks FUSE, the tar.gz: extract it anywhere and run ingetrazo. If something doesn't work, --check tells you what's missing. On macOS (Apple Silicon): download the .dmg from Download ▸ macOS, drag IngeTrazo to Applications and the first time open it with right-click ▸ Open (it isn't signed with an Apple account yet). You can also run it from source with 3 commands.

Is it good for 3D printing?

Not its strong suit yet, but it's on the horizon. Today you can export to binary STL and take it to your slicer, and the solids the engine makes are closed; what it doesn't have yet are dedicated 3D-printing tools (wall-thickness checks, overhangs, mesh repair, 3MF). They're planned for the future.

How do I report a problem or contribute?

The project lives on GitHub: open an issue with your sample file, suggest improvements or send code. You can also get in touch by email.
